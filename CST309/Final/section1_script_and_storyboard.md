## Technical Production Notes
* **Audio Track:** Soft, melancholic ambient strings building into a contemplative cello and subtle bass drop during theoretical transitions.
* **Color Grade:** High contrast, cinematic cool tones for archival/theoretical segments; warmer naturalistic tones for present-day camp B-roll.
* **On-Screen Typography:** Clean sans-serif lower thirds (Inter / Montserrat) with formal APA academic citation footers.
