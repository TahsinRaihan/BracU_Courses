--------------------------------------------------------
2. L298N -> MOTORS (4WD chassis, 4 DC gear motors)
--------------------------------------------------------
Your 4WD kit has 4 motors but the L298N only has 2 independent channels,
so each SIDE's two motors are wired IN PARALLEL onto one channel:

   L298N OUT1  ---> Front-Left motor  (+)
   L298N OUT1  ---> Rear-Left motor   (+)   (both LEFT motors tied together)
   L298N OUT2  ---> Front-Left motor  (-)
   L298N OUT2  ---> Rear-Left motor   (-)

   L298N OUT3  ---> Front-Right motor (+)
   L298N OUT3  ---> Rear-Right motor  (+)  (both RIGHT motors tied together)
   L298N OUT4  ---> Front-Right motor (-)
   L298N OUT4  ---> Rear-Right motor  (-)

If a wheel spins the WRONG way in the calibration test, don't rewire -
just swap that channel's two OUT wires (e.g. swap OUT1/OUT2), or flip the
HIGH/LOW logic for that side in the code (explained in sensor_calibration.ino).

--------------------------------------------------------
3. POWER WIRING (IMPORTANT - read before powering on)
--------------------------------------------------------
You have: 2x 18650 (3.7V each) in a 2S holder = ~7.4V pack, plus a charger.

   Battery (+)  ---> L298N "12V" input terminal
   Battery (-)  ---> L298N GND
   L298N GND    ---> Arduino GND            <-- MUST be common, or nothing works
   Battery (+)  ---> Arduino "Vin" pin       <-- powers the Arduino from the same pack
                                                 (do NOT also power Arduino via USB
                                                 while the battery is connected)

   L298N 5V-EN jumper: LEAVE THE JUMPER ON. At 7.4V input the onboard 5V
   regulator on the L298N will supply ~5V logic power to itself and can
   also power the Arduino's 5V rail for the sensors. If you ever see
   flaky/reset behavior under motor load, remove the jumper and instead
   run a wire from Arduino 5V pin to L298N's 5V pin (separates logic
   power from motor noise) - the pin table above already assumes you may
   need to do this.

   All GNDs (Arduino, L298N, both IR sensors, HC-SR04, buzzer) must tie
   back to the SAME ground point. This is the #1 cause of "nothing works."
